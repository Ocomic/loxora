# Xora's picture

`xora.webp` is Xora's portrait in the app (Milestone 14, section 9).

- **Created by:** the decision owner (Ocomic) with ChatGPT (OpenAI) image generation, October 2026.
- **Input:** a text prompt only. No third-party image, photo of a real person, or existing character was used as a template (confirmed by the decision owner on October 6, 2026).
- **Terms:** OpenAI's terms of use assign the rights in generated output to the user. The decision owner contributes the picture to this project under the project's license (Apache-2.0).
- **Processing:** background removed and scaled to 720 × 900 pixels, saved as WebP with transparency. The original stays outside the repository.
